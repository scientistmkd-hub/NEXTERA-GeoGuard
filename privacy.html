PRIVACY

Privacy Policy.

Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

NEXTERA GeoGuard is designed as a personal safety assistance application. We aim to provide users with safety-related tools while respecting user privacy.

2. Information Used by the App

Depending on the features used, NEXTERA GeoGuard may request access to information such as location information, emergency contact information and communication-related permissions.

3. Location Information

Location access may be required for safety-related functionality such as obtaining the device's current location and enabling location-based emergency assistance.

4. Emergency Contact Information

If the user provides an emergency contact, that information may be stored locally on the device for use by the application's emergency features.

5. SMS and Communication

Certain emergency features may require SMS-related permissions. These permissions are used only for functionality supported by the application.

6. Data Storage

Information such as emergency contact details may be stored locally on the user's Android device depending on the implementation of the application.

7. Third-Party Services

The application may interact with platform services or third-party services, such as mapping services, when required for supported functionality.

8. Data Security

We aim to use reasonable technical measures to protect information handled by the application. However, no digital system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

NEXTERA GeoGuard is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children without appropriate supervision or consent.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application, services or legal requirements change.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact the NEXTERA GeoGuard project team.
